# Final Test photo assets

All files in this folder are local, 1024×768 JPEG derivatives reviewed for the
Final Test picture prompts. Source pages, authors, transformations, and
checksums are kept in `src/data/finalTest/finalTestPhotoAssets.js`.

The family and transport photographs are Public Domain or CC0 1.0. The
classroom and unchanged toy photographs are the existing AkinLearning
reviewed photo pack, copied into this folder and normalized to the same 4:3
frame for offline use. The reviewed replacements have these source records:

- `class-pencil.jpg` — CC0 1.0, Pixabay, [Pencil on White Paper](https://www.pexels.com/photo/pencil-on-white-paper-159752/)
- `phonics-brush.jpg` — CC0 1.0, Karolina Grabowska, [Brush painting the white wall](https://www.pexels.com/photo/brush-painting-the-white-wall-6368/)
- `toy-car.jpg` — CC0 1.0, Alf van Beem, [Soft plastic toy car](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Soft_plastic_toy_car.JPG)
- `toy-doll.jpg` — CC0 1.0, Pixabay, [Brown-haired female doll](https://www.pexels.com/photo/brown-haired-female-doll-207923/)
- `toy-plane.jpg` — CC0 1.0, Claudette Gallant, [Toy Airplane](https://www.publicdomainpictures.net/en/view-image.php?image=96408&picture=toy-airplane)
- `toy-robot.jpg` — CC0 1.0, Karolina Grabowska, [Wooden Robot](https://www.pexels.com/photo/wooden-robot-6069/)
- `toy-teddy.jpg` — CC0 1.0, Pixabay, [Gray Bear Plush Toy](https://www.pexels.com/photo/gray-bear-plush-toy-264907/)

Every image is a local raster file with one clear subject, no readable answer
label, arrow, or answer-colour highlight, and a registry entry with its
dimensions, checksum, licence, creator, source URL, and review date.
